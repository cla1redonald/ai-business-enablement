S1: How big is the team, and what lands on it?

S2: Two of us, me and one engineer, for 140 people. What lands is everything: devices, access, the Filestead estate, Loadstone admin, and now anything with AI in the name. I live in the backlog, I won't pretend otherwise.

S1: Talk me through how a new tool or connector gets approved.

S2: Someone emails me, I have a look, if it seems sane and the vendor looks legitimate I say yes. There's no form, and honestly no record beyond the email thread. It's worked because the volume's been low, but the Verrin stuff is changing that, I get a request a week now for some plug-in or connection.

S1: Can we look at the Verrin admin console together? I'd like to see what you can see.

S2: Sure. [screen share] So, this is the whole thing. Usage tab: that's spend, this month, last month. That's it. I can't tell you usage by person or team, can't see what anyone connected, can't see activity. When Elena asks who's using it, I'm guessing from the seat list.

S1: What about the AI already inside the other tools?

S2: Filestead has its assistant thing, Ledgerline just switched theirs on in an update, I think Tallybook has one coming. None of that went through any decision, it just arrived. There's no list of it anywhere. You're right that we should have one, it's an hour with a spreadsheet, it's finding the hour.

S1: Anything running unattended? Scheduled jobs, automations?

S2: One that matters: the overnight rates pull. Our analyst built a script that logs into three carrier sites in the evening and pulls the updated surcharges into the rate cards. It runs on his laptop, under his logins. When he's on leave it doesn't run, and the week he had it off sick, sales quoted Tuesday on Friday's fuel surcharge.

S1: If he left tomorrow?

S2: It dies with him. I know. It's on the list.

S1: What would you need before you'd let an assistant read Loadstone directly?

S2: A grown-up answer on permissions. Loadstone's permissions are coarse, basically everyone operational sees everything, so anything reading it as a person sees it all too. I'd want that thought through per connection, and right now the thinking time doesn't exist. The two of us haven't had a clear day to learn any of this properly since Verrin landed.
