S1: Thanks for making the time, Elena. Quick framing: this is discovery, not assessment. I'm trying to understand how work actually flows. Do you mind if I record it?

S2: Fine by me. So, the picture. We're about 140 people now. Freight forwarding, mostly European road and short-sea, and the growth plan is to win two or three of the larger retail importers next year. The pitch to them is speed and visibility, and honestly our own back office is neither at the moment.

S1: Where does it pinch first?

S2: Finding things. If I had a pound for every time someone asks where the current rate card is, or which version of a customer's SOP we're working to. We moved everything into Filestead eighteen months ago and it's a skip, not a filing cabinet. That's the complaint I hear most.

S2: The other one is that everything is typed twice. A quote becomes a booking becomes an invoice and I'm fairly sure the same numbers get keyed into three systems on the way. Sales will tell you a quote takes about 20 minutes. The margin on a groupage shipment doesn't survive an hour of admin.

S1: What have you already got running on the AI side?

S2: We put Verrin in on the team plan, must be sixty-odd seats now. Uptake is real, people like it. What I can't tell you is what we get for it. The bill turns up, it's gone up again, and nobody can tell me whose usage that is or what it saved. I'm not against spending it. I'd just like to know what it buys.

S2: And there are a couple of migrations in flight you should know about. We're moving the sales pipeline off spreadsheets into Tallybook, that's half done. And operations keep threatening to upgrade Loadstone, our transport management system, though nothing's scheduled.

S1: If you could unblock one thing and everything else stayed the same?

S2: The reporting. Every Monday I get a margin pack that Douglas's team has hand-built from exports, and by Wednesday I find out a number in it was wrong. I don't blame them, the tooling is spreadsheets and patience. But I'm making pricing calls on it.

S1: Who else should I be talking to?

S2: Aileen for operations, Douglas in finance, Tessa's customer service team, Morag on sales, Marcus for IT. And sit with the customs desk, Femi will show you Portwick. The people doing the work will tell you more than I can.
