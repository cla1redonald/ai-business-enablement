Quote request to booked shipment

From a quote request landing in the sales inbox to a booking confirmed in the TMS, in 6 steps: how this work travels today, and where it hurts.

6 steps  ·  pain scored on 0  ·  timing on 3  ·  constraint at step 5. Full reading notes at the foot of the page.
Why that step is the constraint

Step 5, booking entry into the TMS, is the constraint.

  • The evidence: the only step paid on every booking: about 20 a day at 10 to 15 minutes each when the email is complete, about 30 when it is not, all self reported.
  • Corroboration: confirmed in the room as the standard practice rather than a bad day, and the operations lead weighs it as the constant cost against the episodic carrier waits.
  • The effect: it is the last working step before confirmation, so its delay lands on every booking and cannot be absorbed downstream.
  • What would strengthen the call: a measured timing. Every figure on this map is self reported, so the call rests on consistent tellings rather than measurement.
  • Also unmeasured: no waits were recorded on steps 1 to 5; the one recorded wait (carrier confirmation, step 6) sits after the constraint. That absence reflects what the capture recorded rather than a guarantee nothing was missed.
1

Quote request received and logged

sales desk

Email

Requests arrive by email nine times out of ten and are logged the same day per the written process note. No timing was given for this step.

2

Rates looked up in the per-lane rate cards

sales desk

Filestead (rate-card spreadsheets)

Repeat lanes are quick; a new lane means hunting for rates. The surcharges these cards depend on arrive via the overnight pull, which runs on one analyst's laptop (see the report's Hosting and identity enabler).

3

Quote built in the template and issued

sales desk

Quote templateVerrin (email drafting)

working time: 45 min, self reportedmaturity: work

About 30 quotes a week. Around 45 minutes for a normal one, about 20 for a repeat lane, over an hour with a new lane or customs complications. The written process note's 30-minute target describes the easy cases, in the sales lead's account. Assistant drafting is already normal for the covering email; rates are deliberately kept away from AI until a scoped, verified read of the rate cards exists.

Opportunities on this step
  • Quote assembly assisted from the rate cards, scoped read-only (gated on the verification pattern).
4

Acceptance forwarded to operations as the whole email chain

sales lead

Email

The agreed details are scattered through the thread (the original request, the revisions, the final rate), so the whole chain is forwarded and operations pull it apart at their end. The CRM migration in flight should carry part of this handoff.

Opportunities on this step
  • A structured quote-to-booking handoff replacing the forwarded chain.
  • A completeness check before the handoff reaches the booking desk.
5

Booking entered line by line into the TMS bottleneck

operations coordinator

LoadstoneEmail

working time: 15 min, self reported

About 20 bookings a day, copied field by field from the email chain. 10 to 15 minutes when complete, about 30 when not; roughly a third arrive missing something, and a mismatched rate is the worst case because the invoice picks it up downstream. A working extract-the-fields prototype already exists on this desk, stranded on one machine.

Opportunities on this step
  • Booking entry from forwarded email to confirmed TMS fields (the existing prototype, through the publish route).
  • The completeness check, which removes the 30-minute tail.

How to read this map
What is measured here, and what is not. 0 of 6 steps carry a pain score. 3 carry a timing or wait figure, all self reported. This is a flow with the measurements that exist attached, not a full value stream map. Nothing has been estimated to fill a gap, so a step with no figures had none in the evidence. Maturity tags show where AI already sits in a step today: think means AI at this step is still an idea being talked about, work means AI is used in doing the step, and act means AI already takes part of the action itself.
