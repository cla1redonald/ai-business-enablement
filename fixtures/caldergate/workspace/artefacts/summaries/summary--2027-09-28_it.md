# Call summary: IT

**Date:** 2027-09-28 · **Sub-type:** discovery interview (with a directly-observed console review) · **Present:** [CONSULTANT-1], [IT-LEAD-1]

## What the session covered

The shape of the IT function, the approval path, what the assistant's admin console
can actually show (reviewed together), embedded AI, and the one unattended
automation.

## The function

2 people for 140 staff, carrying devices, access, the file estate, TMS admin and
everything AI. The manager's own words: "I live in the backlog, I won't pretend
otherwise."

## Processes described

- **Approvals.** An email to the IT manager; no form, no record beyond the thread.
  AI-related requests now arrive about weekly.
- **The console (observed directly).** The usage tab shows spend, this month and
  last. No per-user or per-team usage, no activity, no visibility of connections.
- **The overnight rates pull.** A script on one analyst's laptop, under his personal
  logins, feeding the rate cards the official quoting process depends on. It stops
  when he is away; one absence led to quoting on a stale fuel surcharge.

## Embedded AI

Arrives by vendor update with no decision; no list of AI surfaces exists ("it's an
hour with a spreadsheet, it's finding the hour").

## The stated blocker

TMS permissions are coarse ("basically everyone operational sees everything"), named
as what stands between the assistant and the TMS, with no clear time for the
two-person team to think it through per connection.
