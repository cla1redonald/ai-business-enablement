# Call summary: sales

**Date:** 2027-09-27 · **Sub-type:** discovery interview · **Present:** [CONSULTANT-1], [SALES-LEAD-1]

## What the session covered

The quote end to end, the handoff to operations, tenders from larger prospects, and a
deliberate line on AI and rates.

## Processes described

- **Quoting.** About 30 a week, built by hand from per-lane rate-card spreadsheets on
  the shared drive. 45 minutes for a normal one (self reported); about 20 for a
  repeat lane; over an hour for a new lane or customs complications.
- **The handoff.** An accepted quote is forwarded to operations as the whole email
  chain, because the agreed details are scattered through it.
- **Tenders.** A large prospect's tender means two people copying between formats
  for most of a week; about one in four is won.

## Numbers said (verbatim, by role)

- [SALES-LEAD-1]: "45 minutes for a normal one. We do about 30 a week."
- [SALES-LEAD-1]: "That's two people for most of a week, copying between their
  format and ours."

## Contradicts other accounts

Both the sponsor's 20-minute estimate and the process note's 30-minute target are,
in the sales lead's account, descriptions of the easy cases only.

## Tried, and the stated condition

Assistant-drafted quote emails work well. Rates are deliberately kept away from AI
("if it invents a number we eat the difference"); the stated condition for changing
that is a scoped read of "the actual rate cards and only the actual rate cards".
