# Call summary: customer service

**Date:** 2027-09-24 · **Sub-type:** discovery interview · **Present:** [CONSULTANT-1], [CS-LEAD-1], [CS-AGENT-1]

## What the session covered

The status-query day, the escalation dance, cut-off questions, and the FAQ assistant
that died on one wrong answer.

## Processes described

- **Status queries.** About 60 a day across the team; each needs the TMS, the carrier
  portal and usually the email thread. About 5 minutes clean, 20 to 30 with a problem
  (self reported).
- **Escalation.** Typed into an email or phoned to operations, then retyped into TMS
  notes at their end; untrackable once raised.
- **Cut-offs.** The most-asked question; the answer lives in a shared-drive
  spreadsheet that operations sometimes update late.

## Numbers said (verbatim, by role)

- [CS-AGENT-1]: "We take about 60 status queries a day across the team."
- [CS-AGENT-1]: "A clean one, five minutes."

## Signals absent

No churn measure of any kind: "There's no number that would tell you an account's
gone cold until the volume's already gone." ([CS-LEAD-1])

## Tried and abandoned

A FAQ assistant loaded with the FAQ doc and the cut-off spreadsheet. Abandoned after
it gave a customer a stale cut-off, confidently, and the shipment missed the sailing.
The team's own reading: nothing could show its answers were right before they went
out, "and that's what killed it". The demand remains; the lead would use a verified
one "tomorrow".
