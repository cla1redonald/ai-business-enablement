# Call summary: operations team

**Date:** 2027-09-22 · **Sub-type:** discovery interview · **Present:** [CONSULTANT-1], [OPS-LEAD-1], [OPS-COORD-1]

## What the session covered

The booking desk's day: entering confirmed quotes from forwarded email chains into
the TMS, chasing incomplete handoffs, waiting on carriers, and escalating by phone.

## Processes described

- **Booking entry.** About 20 bookings a day arrive as forwarded email chains and are
  copied line by line into the TMS. 10 to 15 minutes each when complete, about 30
  when not (self reported). Confirmed in the room as the standard practice, not an
  exception.
- **Carrier confirmation.** Requests can wait up to 4 hours; nothing moves in the
  window and the customer often chases first.
- **Escalation.** By phone, with the record typed into the TMS after the fact.

## Numbers said (verbatim, by role)

- [OPS-COORD-1]: "Ten to fifteen minutes if the email has everything."
- [OPS-COORD-1]: "Maybe a third arrive missing something."
- [OPS-LEAD-1]: "confirmation can take four hours to come back"

## Handoff pains

The forwarded-chain handoff from sales is the top one; rate mismatches are the worst
form because the invoice picks the rate up downstream.

## Tried and abandoned

[OPS-COORD-1] built a working email-to-TMS-fields assistant tool. It worked on clean
emails, had nowhere to be shared from, and was stopped partly through uncertainty
about whether it was allowed.

## Weighing (the lead's own judgement)

"Honestly the waits hurt more on the bad days, but the keying is every day."
