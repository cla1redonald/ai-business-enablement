# Caldergate Freight AI Business Enablement, V1 baseline

Deliverables from the Fieldnote Advisory AI business enablement engagement, September to October 2027. This
folder is the complete, versioned handover: the report plus the working materials it
draws on. Prepared by the consultant, Fieldnote Advisory. Baselined 2026-10-11.

## Contents

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `Caldergate_Freight_AI_Business_Enablement_Report_V1.md` | The report. Read this first; everything else supports it. |
| `Caldergate_Freight_Opportunity_Workbook_V1.xlsx` | The 21 opportunities, 6 value streams and 6 enabling gaps as data. Arranged so the report's six questions can be run against it directly (the report's annex E maps each question to a column). |
| `value-stream-maps/` | 2 step-level value stream maps as interactive HTML. Open in any browser; no server needed. |
| `enabling-gap-briefs/` | 2 one-page briefs. Each covers what the gap is, what waits on it and what closing it looks like. |
| `call-summaries/` | 7 conversation summaries from the discovery, as markdown. These are the conversation layer of the evidence base. |

## How the pieces join

- The report's section 5 table of 6 enabling gaps matches the Enabling gaps
  sheet in the workbook by ID and name; the 2 briefs in
  `enabling-gap-briefs/` cover the gaps that gate the leading opportunities.
- The 6 streams in the report's section 3.2 tables match the Value streams
  sheet in the workbook by ID and name; the 2 maps in `value-stream-maps/`
  cover the streams mapped at step level.
- The workbook's "Enabling gap dependencies" column joins to the Enabling gaps sheet.
  Blank means the opportunity is open, waiting on nothing.
- The report's annex E maps each of the six choosing-session questions to the workbook
  column that answers it.

## Conventions

- People appear by role, and in the call summaries by bracketed role token (for example
  `[CONSULTANT-1]`); named client organisations appear by neutral token. This is
  deliberate.
- No delivery status is recorded for any opportunity or stream. The evidence describes
  September 2027; re-confirm what is moving before acting on it.
- Blank workbook cells are recorded gaps in the data, not errors.
