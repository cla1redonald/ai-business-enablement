# Guidance for AI agents working in this folder

This is a versioned client deliverable pack: a report and its supporting materials from
an AI business enablement engagement at Caldergate Freight (September to October 2027). Read `README.md`
first for the contents map and how the pieces join.

## Ground rules

- **The report is the authoritative narrative.** The workbook is the authoritative
  data. Where prose and data seem to differ, check the report's annex E and F
  conventions before concluding either is wrong.
- **Join by ID where present, and by exact name otherwise.** Stream names, enabling gap
  names and enabler names are shared identifiers across the report, the workbook
  sheets, the maps and the briefs. Do not paraphrase them when joining or citing.
- **Counts to trust:** 21 opportunities, 6 value streams,
  6 enabling gaps inside 5 enablers, 7 conversations and
  2 documents behind 47 observations. The workbook's stream
  membership counts sum past 21 because 1 opportunity sits on
  more than one stream; that is by design.
- **No delivery statuses.** Nothing in this pack says whether an opportunity is being
  worked on. Do not infer or invent progress; the report tells the reader to re-confirm
  what is moving with the owning teams.
- **Role tokens are deliberate.** People appear as roles or bracketed tokens (for
  example `[CONSULTANT-1]`), client organisations as neutral tokens. Do not attempt to
  resolve them to named individuals.
- **Blank cells in the workbook are recorded gaps in the data, not errors.**
  1 opportunity has no impact or effort grade, 1 has no function, 3 sit on no stream.
- **The evidence ages.** It describes September 2027. The durable parts are the root causes,
  the five enablers and the six questions; treat specific statuses and figures as a
  snapshot.

## Useful entry points

- Run the choosing session: report section 6 plus the workbook, using annex E's
  question-to-column map.
- Understand one stream: its map in `value-stream-maps/`, its row on the workbook's
  Value streams sheet, and its definition in the report's annex A.
- Understand one enabling gap: its brief in `enabling-gap-briefs/`, its row on the
  workbook's Enabling gaps sheet, and the report's section 5.
