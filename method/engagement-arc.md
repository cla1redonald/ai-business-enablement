# The engagement arc

Two shapes: the six-week reference arc (proven end to end) and the two-week fast
diagnostic (the kit's primary scenario; its day-by-day runbook is written against this
arc in `fast-path-runbook.md` once Phase C drills it). Both deliver the same spine:
intake conversations and documents in, evidence-bound observations, opportunities on
value streams, the 20-area readiness assessment rolled into five enablers, a report
whose In brief can stand alone, a versioned pack, and a choosing session booked.

## The six-week reference arc

| Weeks | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1 | Kickoff, access chase, **every interview invite out**, first 3 to 4 discovery sessions, drop-in 1 (the taster plus the topic menu poll) |
| 2 to 3 | Bulk interviews, departmental sessions, drop-in 2, first quick-win pairing |
| 3 to 4 | Mop-up interviews, readiness examination (console reviews, policy reads), drop-in 3, sync with any in-flight client initiative that overlaps the discovery |
| 5 | Synthesis: observations, opportunities, streams, the readiness ratings, the bridge |
| 6 | Report drafting, checking and review cycle; pack build; delivery and the choosing-session offer |

## Pacing rules (learned the expensive way)

- **Interview throughput is the project.** Nothing else can start until sessions are in
  the diary, holidays eat weeks, and a dozen leads need booking. Invites go out in week
  one or the schedule never recovers.
- **Track days as a budget, not a rhythm.** On a fixed-scope engagement, log days used
  after every working session; unused days are not billable later and overrun comes out
  of margin.
- **Ring-fence a full day for the readout.** The readout sells whatever comes next; it
  must not become whatever time is left over.
- **The proof point lands earlier than the readout.** A wary client decides whether the
  work is landing within the first fortnight. Front-load visible value: the first
  drop-in, a first quick win, a first playback.
- **Deliver quick wins during discovery.** Pairing on something real does more for
  acceptance than any argument in the report, and it becomes the report's own momentum
  evidence.
- **Let drop-ins land twice.** Visible demand beyond the sponsor is the strongest asset
  and the most likely to be squeezed; the sponsor should see the room engage at least
  twice before the readout.
- **Ask what the client must protect.** Stretched teams are a design constraint, not an
  obstacle; a quick win reshaped to near-zero cost for a protected team is a better
  quick win.
- **Same-hour debriefs or nothing.** Capture after every call before the next one
  starts. Recollection written immediately and marked as recollection is usable;
  anything later is fiction with confidence.
- **Record redundancy for meetings that matter.** Decide before important calls which
  single recorder is on; when recording fails, ask the client for their own summary,
  which is also better evidence than your recollection.
- **An early playback buys trust.** End of week two: a bucketed playback of interview
  themes, cut by effort to unblock (self-serve, assisted, needs investment), never by
  the consultant's own skillset. Hold value and feasibility on separate axes, and frame
  quick wins as proof the approach works, not as the prize.

## The two-week fast diagnostic (the frame; runbook follows in Phase C)

| Days | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1 to 2 | Intake: kickoff, doc ask, all interviews booked, first sessions run, summaries same-day |
| 3 to 5 | Remaining interviews; observations and candidate opportunities as they land |
| 6 to 8 | Readiness interviews and direct examination (console, policies, connector list) |
| 8 to 9 | Landscape: streams, grades, gates, the bridge |
| 9 to 10 | Report from the skeleton, checks, pack, choosing session booked |

What the fast path cuts from the six-week version: step-level maps for the top 3 to 5
streams only, drop-ins optional (one taster at most), briefs only for gaps that gate the
top opportunities. What it never cuts: real transcripts over summaries, the readiness
instrument run whole, the facts manifest, the checker, the versioned pack, the In brief
written first.

## The deliverable set

One pack (see `templates/pack-readme.md`): report, workbook, maps, briefs, call
summaries, README and agent-facing CLAUDE.md, baselined immutably and zipped. The
choosing session is offered for the week after circulation, facilitated, on the client's
own workbook.
